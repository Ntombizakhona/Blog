Abstraction Obsession
=====================

Cloud Glossary Series Update
----------------------------

A quick announcement: [**my Cloud Glossary is continuing.**](https://medium.com/list/cloud-glossary-528956a3c181)

Of all the articles I have written in the series, my favourite is still [**Abstraction**](https://medium.com/abstraction-d133bec022e).

And honestly, I think I’ve finally figured out why.

**Once you truly understand Abstraction, you start to understand and appreciate Cloud Computing (well and Artificial Intelligence, way more)**

I might even go as far as saying:

> **_Read the Abstraction article properly and you might just master the mental model behind cloud computing._**

Not because one article can teach you every cloud service that exists.

But because it gives you a way of **looking at the cloud.**

Servers become instances.
Infrastructure becomes services.
Complex systems become configurations, dashboards and API calls.

The complexity doesn’t disappear.

**It gets abstracted.**

Certification Across the Clouds
-------------------------------

This has become particularly obvious to me as I’ve been getting certified [**across the clouds**.](https://medium.com/studentanalyst/towards-multicloud-26ad5c69f4d5)

AWS. Azure. GCP. Oracle. And more to come. I kind of want to see what Huawei is all about. I haven’t had much time really. Sad. Speaking of sadness, so far I am dissapointed with IBM.

AWS, despite it all, is still the most elite provider, with GCP coming in second, it has truly evolved over the last two years. Azure and IBM are obsessed with licences, so yeah, not adhering to the whole consumption based model of cloud computing thing.

Anyways, every cloud has its own terminology, consoles and services, but when I strip away the branding, I keep finding the same fundamental ideas underneath.

Compute.

Storage.

Networking.

Databases.

Security.

Scaling.

Automation.

And, of course…

**Abstraction:**

> **_Every Cloud Provider wants you to master their Managed Services._**

That’s one of the reasons I created the Cloud Glossary in the first place.

I’m using it to continuously review the concepts I’m learning while studying for certifications and building things, and stuff like that.

Writing the term down is one thing.

**Explaining it in my own words is another.**

_And that process is helping me cement my cloud knowledge across the different platforms._

Which, honestly, is a fun way to kill multiple birds with one stone.

Or rather acquire multiple certifications, across multiple cloud providers…with one **Glossary!**

The Abstraction Obsession
-------------------------

The funny thing is that once you understand abstraction, you start seeing it everywhere.

In software.

In language.

In people.

In the models we use to understand the universe.

We constantly take something enormously complicated and create a simpler representation that we can actually work with.

That’s exactly what the cloud does.

It lets us interact with immense complexity through simpler layers.

And once you see that, all those cloud services start making a lot more sense.

Once again, **abstraction** is my favourite term right now.

Because if I had to pick one concept that best explains why cloud computing works the way it does…

**it’s abstraction.**

All in all, I just wanted to say, or write, or type, or communicate that I have not forgotten about my Glossary.
